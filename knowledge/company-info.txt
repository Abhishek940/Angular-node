Shopify Company Information
Company Name

The company name is Shopify.

1. When was the company established?

Shopify was established in 2006. The company was co-founded earlier in September 2004, when Tobias Lütke and his co-founders began developing the platform. Shopify's commerce platform was released in 2006.

2. Where is the company located?

Shopify is headquartered in Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Its registered office is at 151 O'Connor Street, Ground Floor, Ottawa, Ontario, Canada. Shopify describes itself as a global company serving merchants around the world.

3. What does the company do?

Shopify is a commerce platform that helps businesses start, run, and grow their businesses. Businesses can use Shopify to create online stores, sell products online and in physical stores, manage products and inventory, process payments, and manage their commerce operations.

4. What services does the company provide?

Shopify provides several commerce-related services, including:

Online store creation
Website and store themes
Product and inventory management
Online checkout
Shopify Payments
Point-of-sale (POS) services for physical stores
Marketing tools
Sales analytics
Shipping and order fulfillment tools
Multiple sales channels
Shopify App Store integrations
24/7 customer support
Tools for small businesses, entrepreneurs, and large enterprises
5. Who is the founder of the company?

Tobias (Tobi) Lütke is a co-founder of Shopify. He co-founded Shopify in September 2004 and has served as the company's CEO since April 2008.

6. What is the company's mission?

Shopify's mission is "to make commerce better for everyone." The company focuses on helping entrepreneurs and businesses start, scale, and grow by providing accessible commerce technology and tools.

7. Tell me about the company.

Shopify is a global commerce technology company founded in Canada. It provides an all-in-one platform that allows entrepreneurs, small businesses, retailers, and large brands to sell products online and in physical stores.

Businesses can use Shopify to create an online store, add and manage products, accept payments, manage inventory, market their products, analyze sales, and sell through multiple channels. Shopify's platform is used by millions of businesses in more than 175 countries.

Shopify was created after its founders wanted a better way to sell products online. The company has since grown into a major global commerce platform whose goal is to make commerce better for everyone.
